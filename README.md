## Movie-Ratings-Analysis-( Numpy, Pandas, Matplotlib )
The project is a Python-based data analysis project that uses NumPy, Pandas, and Matplotlib to study a movie dataset. It focuses on cleaning the data, analyzing movie ratings, and finding patterns based on genre, director, year, budget, and revenue.

# Project Objective
The main objective of this project is to analyze and visualize movie data to understand trends and relationships in movie ratings.

# Business Question
- Which movie genres have the highest average ratings?
- Which directors consistently produce highly rated movies?
- How have movie ratings changed over the years?
- Does a higher movie budget lead to a higher rating?
- Is there a relationship between movie revenue and ratings?
- Which movies are the top-rated movies in the dataset?
- Which genre or director has the strongest overall performance?

# Process
- Handle missing values, duplicates, and incorrect data to prepare the dataset for analysis.
- Use NumPy to calculate mean, median, and standard deviation of ratings, budget, and revenue.
- Use Pandas to calculate average ratings by genre, director, and year.
- Analyze whether budget and revenue are related to movie ratings.
- Use Matplotlib to create bar plots, line plots, scatter plots, and horizontal bar plots.

# Business Insight
- Genre Performance: Some movie genres achieve consistently higher average ratings, helping producers identify genres that audiences appreciate.
- Director Performance: Directors with higher average ratings can be considered strong choices for future projects.
- Rating Trends: Analyzing ratings across years helps identify changes in audience preferences over time.
- Budget vs. Rating: A higher production budget does not necessarily guarantee a higher movie rating.
- Revenue vs. Rating: Movies with higher ratings may have better audience acceptance and can potentially generate stronger revenue.
- Top-Rated Movies: Identifying highly rated movies can help understand the characteristics of successful and well-received films.

# Conclusion
The Movie Ratings Analysis project successfully analyzes movie data using Python, Pandas, NumPy, and Matplotlib. The analysis provides insights into movie ratings based on genre, director, year, budget, and revenue.

The project shows that movie success cannot be determined by a single factor. By analyzing different factors together, production companies can better understand audience preferences, identify successful trends, and make more informed decisions about future movie investments and production.
